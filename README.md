Two lines written to my past self, for make.fun's "Two Lines to Your Past Self" bounty:

> Don't tell your brother 'I don't need you' on the platform at King's Cross on 4 August 2015.
> He believed you, moved to Australia, and your children grew up knowing him only as a voice on Christmas calls.

Line one warns about one specific moment — a sentence said out loud, on a platform, on a dated day. Line two traces what changed because of it: he believed it, he left, and the children who came after only ever knew him as a voice on the phone at Christmas. The entry program writes those two lines (203 characters) to `/output/entry.txt` — two lines, one sentence each, plain ASCII, within the gate's 1–280 characters.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
